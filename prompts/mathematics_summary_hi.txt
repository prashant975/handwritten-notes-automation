Task
Create a student-friendly concise summary from a Mathematics lecture PPT/PDF for Grade 11 & 12 (and JEE/NEET) students from small Indian cities who study in Hindi.
Write the notes in clear, simple Hindi (Devanagari). Keep all mathematical terms, symbols, and formulas as-is. Follow every rule strictly.

Slide-filtering rules [NON-NEGOTIABLE]
1. Exclude housekeeping content: QR codes, ads, homework banners, welcome/thank-you pages, promotion pages, app download lines, etc.
2. Exclude ALL questions and solved problems (MCQs, exercises, illustrations, worked-out solutions and step-by-step working). A Concise Note is a concept summary only.
3. If a slide contains both instructional text and questions, keep only the instructional text.

What to capture
- Definitions, theorems, properties, standard results, and formulas.
- Standard forms and key derived formulas (state them; do not derive them step by step).
- The conditions/cases under which a formula or theorem applies.

Formula rules
- Write formulas in plain text: use "_" for subscripts and "^" for superscripts (e.g. a_n, x^2, nCr, x_1), and write symbols directly (√ × ÷ ≤ ≥ ≠ ± ∞ Σ ∫ π θ). Never use LaTeX, $...$, code fences, or markdown emphasis (* or **) inside a formula.

Language & terminology
- Keep mathematical terms, symbols, variable names, and formulas exactly as they appear; write the surrounding explanation in easy Hindi.

Compression target
Final summary should be about 10-15% of the total instructional content after filtering.

Diagram policy
When a concept needs a diagram/graph from a slide, add this exact note immediately after the related bullet:
(Note to DTP: Insert the image with "<label1>" and "<label2>" given on slide no. <Slide#> under the heading "<Heading>".)

Output structure
Concepts Covered in the Class:
• <Concept 1>
• <Concept 2>
• ...

<Main Topic Heading 1>
• Bullet 1 — definition / theorem / key idea.
• Key formula inline if any.
• Bullet 2 — important property or standard result.

<Main Topic Heading 2>
• ...

Style rules
- Preserve slide order.
- Reuse each slide's original heading verbatim.
- Keep bullets short and precise.
- Output only the final summary. No extra commentary, no markdown code fences.
