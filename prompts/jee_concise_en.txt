Task
Create concise, exam-oriented JEE notes from a lecture PPT/PDF for Class 11 & 12 students preparing for JEE Main and JEE Advanced.
Write in clear, simple English. Follow every rule strictly.

Slide-filtering rules [NON-NEGOTIABLE]
1. Exclude housekeeping content: QR codes, ads, homework banners, welcome/thank-you pages, promotion/subscription pages, app-download lines, batch announcements.
2. Exclude jokes, filler chat, and motivational asides that carry no physics/chemistry/maths content.
3. Exclude unsolved questions, MCQs, answer options, and practice/exercise slides.
4. Include a solved example ONLY when the concept cannot be understood without it. Then keep just the key step and the result, never the full arithmetic.
5. If a slide contains both instructional text and questions, keep only the instructional text.

Compression target
Keep roughly 10–15% of the instructional content after filtering. Every bullet must be one short line.

Output structure
Concepts Covered in the Class:
• <Concept 1>
• <Concept 2>

<Main Topic Heading>
• Key concept in short.
• Important formula: [[MATH_INLINE: <latex>]]
• Important point or shortcut/trick.
• Common mistake or exam trap, when the slide shows one.

<Next Heading>
• ...

MATH NOTATION [ABSOLUTELY NON-NEGOTIABLE]
Every formula, symbol, variable with a script, unit with a power, and chemical formula MUST be wrapped in a protected maths tag. Never write these as plain text.

Inline maths (inside a sentence or bullet):
[[MATH_INLINE: latex_here]]

Displayed maths (a standalone equation on its own line):
[[MATH_BLOCK:
latex_here
]]

Write correct LaTeX inside the tags:
- Vectors: [[MATH_INLINE: \vec{A}]], [[MATH_INLINE: \vec{B}]], [[MATH_INLINE: \vec{C}]]
- Cross product: [[MATH_INLINE: \vec{A} \times \vec{B} = \vec{C}]]
- Unit vectors: [[MATH_INLINE: \hat{i}, \hat{j}, \hat{k}]]
- Cyclic order: [[MATH_INLINE: \hat{i} \rightarrow \hat{j} \rightarrow \hat{k}]]
- Dot product: [[MATH_INLINE: \vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = AB\cos\theta]]
- Magnitude: [[MATH_INLINE: |\vec{A} \times \vec{B}| = AB\sin\theta]]
- Superscript: [[MATH_INLINE: x^2]]
- Subscript: [[MATH_INLINE: v_0]]
- Combined: [[MATH_INLINE: a_n = \frac{v^2}{r}]]
- Chemistry: [[MATH_INLINE: H_2O]], [[MATH_INLINE: CO_2]], [[MATH_INLINE: SO_4^{2-}]]
- Units: [[MATH_INLINE: m\,s^{-2}]], [[MATH_INLINE: mol\,L^{-1}]]

NEVER output these:
A x B = C
i -> j -> k
H2O
CO2
ms-2

ALWAYS output these instead:
[[MATH_INLINE: \vec{A} \times \vec{B} = \vec{C}]]
[[MATH_INLINE: \hat{i} \rightarrow \hat{j} \rightarrow \hat{k}]]
[[MATH_INLINE: H_2O]]
[[MATH_INLINE: CO_2]]
[[MATH_INLINE: m\,s^{-2}]]

Preserve the teacher's notation
If the slide shows a vector arrow, cap/hat, bar, dot, subscript, superscript, Greek symbol, angle, degree, plus/minus, root or fraction — reproduce it EXACTLY using LaTeX inside a maths tag.

Do not simplify equations
- Do not turn a formula into words only.
- Do not drop vector arrows or unit-vector hats.
- Do not drop powers or subscripts.
- Do not replace \times with a plain "x" when it means a cross product.

Diagram policy
When a concept needs a diagram, add this exact note immediately after the related bullet:
(Note to DTP: Insert the image with "<label1>" and "<label2>" given on slide no. <Slide#> under the heading "<Heading>".)

Style rules
- Preserve lecture/slide order.
- Reuse the original slide headings verbatim wherever possible.
- Keep JEE-relevant depth: definitions, formulas, conditions of validity, sign conventions, limiting cases.
- Output only the final notes. No commentary, no markdown code fences.
