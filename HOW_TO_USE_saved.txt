========================================================================
 HANDWRITTEN NOTES AUTOMATION  —  HOW TO USE  (Team Guide)
========================================================================

WHAT IT DOES
------------
Upload a lecture PDF or PowerPoint and it generates PW-style "Concise
Notes" as a Word (.docx) and PDF file, with diagrams included.


BEFORE YOU START (one-time)
---------------------------
1. You need a Windows 10 or 11 PC.

2. Your @pw.live Google account must be approved for this app.
   If you see "not authorized" when you sign in, message the person who
   shared this app and ask them to add your @pw.live email to the
   allowlist. (Only @pw.live accounts can use it.)

3. For the PDF file: Microsoft Word OR LibreOffice should be installed
   on the PC. If neither is installed you will still get the editable
   Word (.docx) file — just not the PDF.

4. (Optional) For the real handwritten look, install the free "Kalam"
   font: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Kalam  -> Download -> unzip
   -> select the .ttf files -> right-click -> Install. If you skip this,
   notes still generate, just in a normal font.


INSTALL
-------
1. Unzip "HandwrittenNotesAppTeam_share.zip" to a folder you can find
   (for example, your Desktop).
   IMPORTANT: extract it fully first. Do NOT run the app from inside
   the zip preview window.

2. Open the extracted "HandwrittenNotesAppTeam" folder.


RUN
---
1. Double-click "HandwrittenNotesAppTeam.exe".

2. A black command window opens. After a few seconds your web browser
   opens the app automatically (at http://localhost:8501).
   - Keep the black window OPEN while you use the app. Closing it stops
     the app.
   - The very first launch can take 20-60 seconds. Please be patient.

3. If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box, click
   "More info" -> "Run anyway". The app is safe; it just isn't
   code-signed.


SIGN IN
-------
1. Click "Continue with Google" and sign in with your @pw.live account.
   Google asks you to confirm access the first time — say yes. That is what
   lets the app keep your session alive without asking you to sign in again.
2. If you see "not authorized for this app", your email hasn't been
   added yet — ask the person who shared this app.
3. You stay signed in for 7 DAYS — including after closing and reopening
   the app. The sidebar shows "Auth status: OK" and how many days are left.
   You do NOT need to sign out and back in to fix anything.

4. If you ever see "Session expired" or "reconnect needed", click
   "Reconnect Google" ONCE. Repeatedly signing out and in does not help
   and is never required.

5. Always open the app at  http://localhost:8501
   NOT at http://127.0.0.1:8501 — your browser treats those as two
   different sites, and a sign-in done on one is not recognised on the
   other. The app now warns you if you land on the wrong address.


GENERATE NOTES
--------------
1. Upload one or more files (PDF, PPTX, or PPT).
2. Choose the SUBJECT (Biology, Physics, Chemistry, or Mathematics) and
   the NOTES LANGUAGE (English or Hindi). Both are required.
   - For a Hindi lecture, select "Hindi" here or the notes come out in
     English.
3. Click "Generate Concise Notes" and wait. Large files can take a
   few minutes.
4. For each file you can download:
   - DOCX  (editable Word file)
   - PDF   (needs Word or LibreOffice installed)
   - ZIP   (everything from that run)

   Files are named after your uploaded file, for example an upload named
   "Structure_of_Atom_15.pdf" produces:
       Structure_of_Atom_15_Concise_Notes.docx
       Structure_of_Atom_15_Concise_Notes.pdf


WHERE DO THE FILES GO?
----------------------
Your downloads go to your browser's usual Downloads folder. A copy of
every run is also saved in the "outputs" and "runs" folders that sit
next to the .exe.


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
- "Port 8501 is already in use"
      An old app window is still running. Close ALL
      HandwrittenNotesAppTeam windows, then start it again.

- The browser didn't open
      Open your browser and go to:  http://localhost:8501

- "not authorized for this app"
      Ask the person who shared this app to add your @pw.live email to
      the allowlist.

- "Couldn't verify your access..." / it won't let you in
      Usually a brief network or server hiccup. Wait a moment and click
      again. Make sure you have internet access.

- I only got a DOCX, no PDF
      Install Microsoft Word or LibreOffice, then generate again. The
      DOCX always works.

- The notes are in a normal font, not handwriting
      Install the free "Kalam" font (see step 4 above), then generate
      again.


NEED HELP?
----------
Contact the person who shared this app with you.
