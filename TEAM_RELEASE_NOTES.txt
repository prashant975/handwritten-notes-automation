HANDWRITTEN NOTES AUTOMATION - TEAM RELEASE
Build date: 23 July 2026

PROBLEMS SOLVED
---------------
1. Mathematical equations now use protected math tags and native Word equations
   (OMML/Cambria Math) instead of being forced into the Kalam body font.

2. Vector arrows, unit-vector hats, cross products, dot products, fractions,
   roots, Greek symbols, superscripts and subscripts are preserved.

3. Chemical formulae and scientific units are preserved, including examples
   such as H2O, CO2, SO4^2-, v0, x^2, 10^-3 and m s^-2.

4. Common broken AI formula output is repaired conservatively before DOCX
   creation. Repairs and remaining warnings are recorded in equation reports.

5. JEE, NEET and JEE + NEET concise-note modes are available, with strict
   exam-oriented prompts and removal of repeated or unnecessary teacher talk.

6. QR codes, advertisements, social-media panels, phone-number slides,
   homework/welcome/thank-you slides, ASQ/MCQ slides, answer options and worked
   question images are filtered out.

7. Filtered promotional/question slides can no longer re-enter through diagram
   redraw or a mistaken AI "Note to DTP" request. DOCX insertion performs a
   final independent safety check.

8. The app provides DOCX, PDF, Equation Quality Report and Full Run ZIP
   downloads without regenerating the notes when a download button is clicked.

9. PDF export failure no longer prevents the DOCX and reports from being
   delivered. A clear warning is shown when Word/LibreOffice is unavailable.

10. Authentication/session handling, proxy access checks and usage tracking are
    included in the packaged team application.

TEAM USAGE
----------
